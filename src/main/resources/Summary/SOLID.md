SOLID Principles — Final Summary

S — Single Responsibility Principle
cohesive responsibilities,
avoiding god classes,
separation of concerns.

O — Open/Closed Principle
extension without modification,
strategy-based architectures,
avoiding branching logic.

L — Liskov Substitution Principle
behavioral contracts,
semantic correctness,
truthful abstractions.

I — Interface Segregation Principle
focused capabilities,
avoiding bloated interfaces,
capability-based modeling.

D — Dependency Inversion Principle
depending on abstractions,
stable business logic,
infrastructure isolation.

Most Important Achievement

thinking about contracts,
extension points,
abstractions,
orchestration,
capability modeling,
coupling,
system evolution.

That is MUCH more valuable than memorizing definitions.
